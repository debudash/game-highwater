# River Guard — Level 1 (tutorial) · draft v1

Status: **level-1 spec (Nov 2, 2026).** Game-wide rules (impact pacing, the bomb-hit look, the respawn safety rule, controls, panels) are in `river-guard-rulebook.md`; this doc holds only what is special to level 1: its script, its tutorial aid, its prompts and its stress checks. All numbers are the rulebook's (timing v6: 18×14, S = 60 ms, X = 17, F = 8, H = 14; band-A spawn interval 1,333, cross step 400, invuln 960, boat budget 9,920, drown 9,360). History, the voice-brief corrections and the settled questions are in `river-guard-changelog.md`.

**Nov 2 update:** the level 1 jet is a **cross jet** that may drop **several bombs** while it is on screen, at **random columns across all 18**. One bomb is guaranteed, at a random column. Every other cell rolls the band-A chance. Each bomb falls straight down to the ground and vanishes, or destroys the tank if it hits it (rulebook §4 Jets and Bombs). **Dive jet (Debu, Nov 2, 2026, issue 4 A):** when the cross jet leaves, a second scripted jet dives straight down the tank's column, so level 1 also shows the jet that can hit the tank.

## Terms
- **Cell (c, r):** column 0–17 from the left, row 0–13 from the top (sky 0–8, lane 9, ground 10, river 11–13).
- **Level start:** tick 0, the first simulated tick of level 1 (rulebook §9 *Tick length*: tick index 0 at level start). The tank is placed before tick 0's step 1.
- **t_ms:** game time in ms since level start, always a whole tick: t_ms = 60 × tick index. Every time in this doc is the t_ms of the tick on which the event resolves (tick-exact, Debu, Nov 2, 2026, issue 8 A).
- **Step:** one cell of movement, taken when that entity's step timer fires. The tick order is the rulebook's §9 *Tick order*: input and movement, timers, threat steps, respawn placement, hits, crisis spawns.
- **Sprite → grid:** game logic only. Every entity occupies **exactly one cell**, and its art is drawn inside that cell's tile. The hooked chopper's two-tile sprite (*Sprites needed*) is two entities drawn together, the chopper in its row-11 cell and the boat in its row-12 cell. Tiles are **16×16 LCD px** (Debu, Nov 2). The earlier art sizes (tank 11×5, jet about 9×4 or 4×9) were drawn for 12 px tiles and should be redrawn to fill 16 px. The 3×3 XOR hit flash and the 5×5 blast-off are **cosmetic only**. They are measured in cells around the hit cell, clipped at the edges, and have no collision. Frame times are settled (question 7).
- **Dive-jet state:** the scripted dive jet is `waiting` until the cross jet leaves play, then `pending` from that tick (T_x) until it spawns (normally the next tick, T_x + 60; later while it waits out an impact lock), then `active`, then `gone` once it leaves play (shot, removed on hitting the tank, or removed on ground row 10). It never returns to an earlier state.
- **Jets gone:** the cross jet has left play (exited or shot) **and** the dive jet is `gone`. A `pending` dive jet counts as a jet on screen, so the tick(s) between the cross jet leaving and the dive jet spawning are never "no jet on screen" (Debu, Nov 2, 2026).

**Boat path:** drowning boats **don't move** in v1 (rulebook §4). The only crossing is the chopper's ferry. The art shows the boat sinking or bobbing in place.

**Drown in the tutorial (Debu, Nov 2): replay, and it doesn't count.** If the tutorial boat drowns, `Crisis → None`, `DROWNED` **does not** go up, and the side panel shows `BOAT LOST` and `TRY AGAIN`. If the player was flying when it drowned, they are returned to the tank at their current column on the same tick (rulebook §4 Boats; Debu, Nov 2, 2026), so the replay always starts with the player in the tank. If they were flying, SkyPolicy lifts with the return stampede (rulebook §9), so any bombs held on row 6 resume at full pace, each still 3 bomb steps above the lane. With D the tick of the drowning (or fuel-out), the same boat respawns at (13, 12) in step 6 of tick D + 16 (960 ms later) as a new crisis, never delayed, because column 13 stays bomb-free after T_c (Script, *T_c*), with a fresh drown timer (9,360) and a fresh flight budget (9,920). This repeats until the boat is rescued, so level 1 can't end in a drowned-boat game over. Lives can't run out before the rescue either (*Last-life net*). **Bomb sink (rulebook §4 Boats):** it can't happen in level 1. A bomb enters the river only in the boat's column and only if it was dropped after the boat spawned (rulebook §4 Boats, *Bomb sink* item 1). The tutorial boat spawns only after both jets are gone, so no bomb can be dropped while it exists, and its column is bomb-free (Debu, Nov 2, 2026, issue 5 A). Bombs still falling in other columns reach the ground and vanish. If a later level ever reuses the tutorial boat, a sink is handled like a drowning here: replay, no count. **Fuel out (Debu, Nov 2, 2026, issue 12 A):** while `SAFE BOAT` is on, the flight budget running out ends the attempt at once instead of leaving the boat to drown. On the tick the budget expires (step 2, before the drown timer, rulebook §9 *Tick order*), the player is returned to the tank at their current column (rulebook §9 *Chopper flight timer*, items 3–4), the boat is removed, `Crisis → None`, `DROWNED` does not go up, `BOAT LOST` and `TRY AGAIN` show, and the boat respawns in step 6 of D + 16 exactly as after a drowning. So the dead wait after a spent budget (up to about 9.3 s) is gone, and the state "Tank with the budget spent" can't occur in level 1. If the budget and the drown timer expire on the same tick, it counts as a **fuel-out** (Debu, Nov 2, 2026, final call 4): the budget is checked first in step 2. The outcome is the same either way. A rescue in step 1 of that tick comes first and wins. This is a **tutorial aid**, `SAFE BOAT` (drowning and fuel-out replay without counting), shown in the top panel's `TUTORIAL` field from the first T_b until the tutorial boat is rescued, including the 960 ms gaps between attempts (Debu, Nov 2, 2026, issue 11 A). *Note:* a player who never rescues it replays forever; there's no cap.

**Last-life net (tutorial aid; Debu, Nov 2, 2026, GO-A).** *On:* while lives = 1, from the tick lives become 1 (step 5 of the hit that takes them from 2 to 1) until the rescue. A hit at 3 or 2 lives isn't affected by the net, so this is the same rule as "on from tick 0", written so the `TUTORIAL` label shows exactly while the net is on (Debu, Nov 2, 2026, L-A). *Off:* on the rescue tick R (the first rescue, which is `LEVEL 1 CLEAR`), from step 1 of R on, so every later hit follows the normal rules (a hit at 1 life is then game over). *Rule:* while the net is on, if the one tank hit picked in step 5 of a tick (one life per tick, priority mine, bomb, jet; rulebook §4 Tank) would take lives from 1 to 0, lives stay at **1**. Everything else about the hit is normal: the bomb that hit is removed (a dive jet that hits is removed, no score), the hit look plays, the 420 ms impact lock (`impactLockMs`) and 960 ms invulnerability start on the impact tick, the tank is TankInvuln, and it respawns at T_r by the rulebook §8 *Respawn safety rule*, which applies to this hit as if a life had been lost with lives remaining. Hits at 3 or 2 lives cost a life as usual, so before the rescue lives go 3 → 2 → 1 and then stay at 1. *Unchanged:* score, `DROWNED`, crisis, flight budget and every timer except the hit's own lock and invulnerability. *Panel:* `LIVES` shows 1 (it never shows 0 in level 1). On an absorbed hit the side panel shows the transient `LAST LIFE SAFE` **instead of** `LIFE LOST` (*Side message panel*). *Dive jet:* its "no spawn at 0 lives" case can't occur, so it always spawns; its lock wait happens at most once, because the wait ends at T_r + 60 = impact + 480, inside that hit's invulnerability, so no new lock can start before it spawns. *Boat timing:* unchanged (T_b ≤ 16,920). *Label:* `LAST LIFE SAFE` (*Top status panel*, *Tutorial aids in level 1*).

**Clear condition:** the tutorial boat reaches a bank while hooked, so `Crisis` goes from Boat to None by rescue. Both jets are gone at that point; bombs may still be held on row 6, and they resume after the rescue. Shooting the jets is optional (+100 each).

**After `LEVEL 1 CLEAR` (Debu, Nov 2, 2026, issue 6 A; details final call 3):** the run carries on into normal band-A play with the score kept (100–700) and **lives carried over unchanged**. With R the rescue tick, the normal jet spawner's accumulator starts at 0 on tick R and gains 60 on each later tick, so the first normal jet spawns in step 3 of tick R + 23, **1,380 ms** after the rescue (the first tick with ≥ 1,333 elapsed). Bombs held on row 6 resume with the return stampede. The level-1 script, its prompts and `SAFE BOAT` end on the rescue tick, and from then on only the rulebook applies: the normal jet spawner runs, boats come from the crisis scheduler once the score reaches `boatUnlockScore` (2,000), and mines once it reaches `mineUnlockScore`. The guaranteed first-jet drop doesn't repeat, because the level-1 cross jet was the run's first jet. A restart after game over always begins a new run at level 1 (rulebook §5 *Restart*).

## Stress check
- **Bomb vs dodge:** the bomb is visible from row 1 for 5,280 ms (8 bomb steps) before it reaches the lane. A one-cell dodge is immediate on a press (a held key repeats every 120 ms; rulebook §7). ✓ The cross jet stays on row 0 and the bomb is on rows 1–10, so they never share a cell. ✓

Lives = 0 needs a −1 at 1 life, which the last-life net blocks until the rescue, and tutorial drownings don't count (`SAFE BOAT`), so **a game over before the rescue is impossible** for any input. *Idle player:* an idle tank stays at column 8. *Cross jet:* it is hit only by a bomb dropped over column 8: the guaranteed one lands there in 1 of 18 runs, otherwise that cell rolls 0.10, so 15% of runs (1/18 + 17/18 × 0.10), at most once (tick-exact 9,900 from the left, 10,260 from the right); the respawn 420 ms later is at column 8, which is safe because the dive jet hasn't spawned or is still ≥ 4,000 ms from the lane. *Dive jet:* it spawns over column 8 at 8,640, so the idle tank loses **exactly one** life to it: the first of its bombs to reach the lane (13,920–14,400), or the jet itself at 15,120 if it dropped none (0.9^9 = 38.7%). After a bomb hit, the respawn 420 ms later finds column 8 unsafe (the jet reaches row 9 within its 2,160 ms window), so the tank goes to column 9 and the jet and its later bombs miss. Column 9 is safe because every cross bomb has left by 14,160. *Result:* an idle player takes 1 hit (85.0%) or 2 (15.0%), never 3, all before T_b; every bomb is gone by then, because the dive jet is the last to leave. - **Last-life net, adversarial (Debu, Nov 2, 2026, GO-A):** *Loops:* none. A net hit is a normal hit, so it removes its bomb or jet and starts a 960 ms invulnerability; level 1 has finitely many threats (one cross jet, one dive jet, at most 27 bombs), so hits can't repeat forever, and once both jets and all bombs are gone nothing can hit. *Stall:* the net adds none; the only endless case is the existing one, a player who never rescues the boat replays forever (`SAFE BOAT`, no cap). *T_b:* bounded at 16,920, as before. The net doesn't add threats, and the dive-jet lock wait happens at most once (*Last-life net*). *Rules that assume lives can hit 0:* the rulebook §4 Tank / §8 hit row ("if lives 0 → game over") can't fire before the rescue. The dive jet's "no spawn at 0 lives" case is unreachable. The §8 *Respawn safety rule* says it applies "after a life loss when lives remain", and the net states that an absorbed hit respawns by that rule too. The §5 "same-frame both" tie can't occur (no drowned count in level 1). ✓

Pinned lines are at most 2 (`TUTORIAL BOAT` and one step prompt; neither shows in the 960 ms gaps). Transients fill the rest newest first, and the oldest transient is dropped when a 5th line would appear. So the layout on every tick follows from the rules. The worst burst is a drowning while in the tank and a hit on the same tick: `BOAT LOST`, `TRY AGAIN` (step 2), then `LIFE LOST` or `LAST LIFE SAFE` (step 5), with no pinned lines in the gap: 3 of 4 lines. With a boat on screen (2 pinned lines), no `+100` can come, because both jets are gone before T_b. The only transients that can arrive within 960 ms of an absorbed hit are `BOAT LOST` and `TRY AGAIN` from a drowning or fuel-out, and that event unpins both pinned lines. So `LAST LIFE SAFE` is never dropped. A second `+100` within 960 ms of the first (cross jet shot, then the dive jet shot quickly) is a repeat: one line, with its hold restarted (rulebook §3 rule 1). *Width:* `LAST LIFE SAFE` is 14 characters, shorter than the longest approved side-panel string, `PRESS DOWN: CHOPPER` (19), so it fits wherever those already fit. ✓
- **Hook vs drown:** the worst hook to column 13 is notice 480 + 13×240 = 3,600. The board-wide worst is 4,560. Drown is 9,360, leaving ≥ 4,800 spare. ✓ Bombs still falling elsewhere don't change this: Down is accepted at once, and in the chopper nothing can cost a life. The one delay is a tank hit just before T_b: its 420 ms impact lock ignores Down, which still leaves ≥ 4,380 spare.
- **Budget vs run:** the worst run is a 13-cell drop plus a ferry the wrong way to column 0, which rescues on T + 153 against a budget that runs out on T + 165: 720 ms spare, tick-exact (Script, *T_b …*). ✓ The intended run is 5 + 4 cells, or 1,200 + 1,920 = 3,120 ms. A slower run ends by fuel-out and replays (`SAFE BOAT`). Falling bombs don't touch the budget, which drains only while flying.
- **Rescue rules:** `PlayerMode ∈ Chopper* ∧ Crisis = Boat`, so SkyPolicy is on (ceiling row 6, rpf 0.375). Under 5A both jets are gone but bombs may still be falling in other columns, so the rescue is taught **without jets** but not always without bombs. On the first Down of each tutorial boat (each replay is a new crisis), the rescue-start clear removes every bomb on rows 7–10, and bombs on rows 1–6 step down at rescue pace and are held on row 6, where they can't cost a life (rulebook §9 *SkyPolicy*). They resume at full pace with the return stampede when SkyPolicy lifts: after the rescue (normal play, *After `LEVEL 1 CLEAR`*), or after an Up, a drowning or a fuel-out. None of them can reach the boat, because column 13 is bomb-free. Crisis XOR holds trivially (no mines). The Down gate and the Up-while-hooked drop follow the rulebook unchanged; budget expiry ends the attempt (*Fuel out*, `SAFE BOAT`).
- **Hook predicate (settled, Debu, Nov 2):** hover over the boat's column and press Down. It's column overlap, so the worst-hook formula still counts columns only. The Down press costs one reaction, about 200–500 ms, covered by the ≥ 4,800 ms drown spare and, on the worst run, by the 480 ms free hook window plus 720 ms budget spare (Script, *T_b …*).

## Impact pacing and respawn (level 1)
Impact pacing (`impactLockMs` = 420), the bomb-hit look and the respawn safety rule are game-wide and live in the rulebook: §4 Tank (*Impact pacing*, *Bomb hits the tank*) and §8 *Respawn safety rule* (Debu, Nov 2, 2026, issue 7 A). Level 1 uses them unchanged.

**Level-1 effect:** for an idle player the rule moves the tank at most once. A cross-jet bomb hit respawns at column 8 (the only cross bomb that can hit column 8 is the one that just hit it, and the cross jet never returns). A hit by one of the dive jet's bombs respawns at column 9, because the dive jet is about to reach column 8. **An idle player loses 1 life before the boat (85% of runs) or 2 (15%), never 3** (*No game over before the rescue*, above). A player who has moved may get a respawn shifted by other falling bombs, which is the rule doing its job. It matters most from level 2 on, when more dive jets can be heading for the lane.
